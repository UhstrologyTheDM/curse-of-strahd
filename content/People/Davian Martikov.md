# At a Glance
A gruff, no nonsense elderly man, the leader of the Martikov family. Also the leader of the Keepers of the Feather, a network of wereraven spies across Barovia that oppose Strahd.

Has asked you to escort her to St. Andral's church in Vallaki, beyond the view of Castle Ravenloft and (hopefully) beyond the reach of Strahd. [[Father Donavich]] told you of another option: The Abbey of St Markovia in Krezk. A striking girl, decent with a sword, sharp-witted, and terrified of Strahd. She wants nothing more than to put the past behind her and live out her days in hiding.

# Lore
The sister of the current Burgomaster of Barovia, and current desire of Strahd. Strahd charmed his way into the mansion, killed her father, and bit Ireena, but didnt turn her. 

She has been dying her hair black with a mix of charcoal, since being bitten. Also travels with a red scarf around her neck.

Met with the Baron to negotiate the refugees entrance into the town.
